## Overview
**PT Panorama Varia Cipta** is an established manufacturing and distribution enterprise specializing in modern architectural PVC ceilings, wall paneling, and interior building materials. This project involved designing and engineering the company's high-conversion web profile and digital product catalogue CMS to showcase product lines, specifications, and distributor networks across Indonesia.

## Technical Architecture
Adapting our battle-tested CMS engine, the architecture consists of:
- **Client Presentation Tier (React JS):** Clean, modern corporate aesthetic highlighting high-resolution architectural installation photography, interactive product specs, and direct WhatsApp / RFQ (Request for Quotation) triggers.
- **Content Engine & Admin (Laravel + Livewire):** Rapid administrative portal for product catalogue management, spec sheets upload, project gallery curation, and inquiry tracking.
- **Relational Data Schema (MySQL):** Structured database modeling product categories, pattern variants, dimensions, and technical certifications.

## Key Features
- **Dynamic Architectural Product Showcase:** Searchable, category-filtered catalogue displaying panel dimensions, textures, finishes, and sample installations.
- **Inquiry & Lead Capture:** Seamless integration with company sales channels, routing visitor inquiries with pre-filled product codes.
- **Case Study / Installation Portfolio:** Gallery module allowing the sales team to publish completed residential and commercial installation showcases.
- **Lightweight SEO & Performance:** Fast load times on mobile connections for construction contractors and architectural clients on site.

## Engineering Takeaways
Refactoring and adapting the CMS architecture from prior projects demonstrated the value of reusability, clean modular design, and robust schema abstractions for corporate web applications.
